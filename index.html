High five!

A self-contained HDF5 browser with signal-processing and plotting tools. Works offline; files never leave your computer.

Workflow

  • Open one or more files (button or drag & drop). Click groups to expand; the Info tab shows metadata and attributes, Data shows values as a table.
  • Press + next to a dataset (or double-click it for a quick plot) to add it as a component. Components get letters A, B, C…
  • Use the Slice field with numpy syntax: : all, 5 one index, 100:2000, ::10 every 10th, :, 2 column 2, 0, :, : first frame of a cube. Fixed indices get sliders.
  • The processing panel (sliders icon) applies, in order: scale × / offset +, detrend, Butterworth filter (zero-phase optional), smoothing, then an operation (derivative, integral, dB, normalize…).
  • Choose the X axis: sample index, a generated time axis (t₀ + sample rate or step), or another component. Its spacing defines the sample rate used by filters and spectra; you can override it.
  • For 2-D components, choose which dimension the signals run along — each column (or row) becomes its own trace.

Plot types

  • Line / Scatter – Y components against the X axis. Plot one component against another to get XY curves; mismatched sizes are flagged and truncated.
  • Spectrum – Welch PSD / ASD / power spectrum (segment length, FFT samples for zero-padding, overlap, window, mean or median averaging) or a single-FFT amplitude spectrum, optional dB.
  • Spectrogram, Histogram.
  • Colormap, 3D surface, 3D scatter – Z from a 2-D component (with optional X/Y coordinate vectors) or three 1-D components (regular grids are detected; scattered points are binned).

Mouse

  • 2-D: drag to box-zoom (a thin horizontal drag zooms X only, vertical zooms Y only), Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom (Shift: X only, Alt: Y only), double-click to reset, click legend entries to hide series.
  • Touch: drag to pan (rotate in 3-D), pinch to zoom (spread fingers horizontally for X only, vertically for Y only), tap for a readout or to toggle a legend entry, double-tap to reset. On phones, use the bottom bar to switch between Files, Plot and Settings; double-tap a dataset for a quick plot.
  • 3-D: drag to rotate, Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, double-click to reset.

Format support

  • HDF5 superblock v0–v3 (incl. userblocks), old- and new-style groups (compact & dense), soft & external links (shown), contiguous, compact and chunked storage with every chunk index type.
  • Filters: gzip/deflate, shuffle, Fletcher-32, LZF, LZ4. Not supported: szip, blosc, zstd, bitshuffle, virtual datasets.
  • Types: integers, floats (16/32/64), strings, enums, compound (pick a field), arrays, variable-length. NetCDF-4 files are HDF5 and open too. Large files are read in pieces, so they need not fit in memory.
